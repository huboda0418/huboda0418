## 📌 Current Research

I am currently working on **dynamic 3D Gaussian SLAM**, with a focus on improving visual localization robustness and compact 3D Gaussian mapping in dynamic scenes.

### RPL-GC

**Risk-Guided Parent-Lineage Growth Control for Dynamic 3D Gaussian SLAM**

My current research investigates parent-lineage-based risk modeling and Gaussian growth control for dynamic scenes, aiming to suppress redundant Gaussian growth while maintaining robust camera localization.
